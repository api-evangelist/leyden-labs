LEIDEN, The Netherlands. April 8, 2025: Leyden Laboratories B.V. (the “Company” or “Leyden Labs”) today announced the publication of new findings that showcase the advantages of intranasal administration of monoclonal antibodies over systemic administration for protection against airborne viruses.
